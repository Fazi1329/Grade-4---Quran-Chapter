# 🕌 Grade 4 Islamic Studies Interactive Quiz: The Qur'an (Chapter 7)

An elegant, child-friendly, fully interactive quiz built for Grade 4 students, based strictly on **Chapter 7: The Qur'an (اَلْقُرْآن)**, Pages 27–31 of the Islamic Studies textbook.

## 🌟 Key Highlights

- **Islamic Theme & Child-Friendly Aesthetics:** Calming emerald green (`#0f6244`), radiant gold (`#d4a017`), white, and light-blue palette with swaying lanterns (*fawanees*), crescent moons, stars, and Bismillah calligraphy.
- **Accurate Chapter Content:** 100% sourced from Pages 27–31 of the textbook covering Prophets, Holy Books, the first revelation in the Cave of Hira, Surah divisions, preservation, and Hadith teachings.
- **Interactive Audio Effects (Web Audio API):** Custom melodic synthesizer chimes for correct answers, gentle hint tones, and celebratory fanfares. No external audio files or downloads required!
- **Dual Drag-and-Drop + Tap-to-Match:** Works seamlessly on desktop mouse interactions, Chromebooks, iPads, tablets, and smartphones.

## 📋 Quiz Structure (28 Total Marks)

### 📝 Section A: Answer the Following Questions (8 Marks)
- **Q1 (3 Marks):** Why do you think Allah sends Prophets to mankind? *(Page 27 & Page 31, Ex. A.1)*
- **Q2 (3 Marks):** The Qur'an is the most precious gift to mankind. Explain why. *(Page 27, 28, 30 & Page 31, Ex. A.2)*
- **Q3 (2 Marks):** Describe what happened when Angel Jibreel (عليه السلام) first appeared to Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ) in the Cave of Hira. *(Page 27 & Page 29)*
- *Features:* Concept-based auto-evaluator with tolerance for Grade 4 spelling variations, instant feedback badges, and textbook model answers.

### ⚡ Section B: One-Word Answers (8 Marks)
1. The Angel of revelation: **Jibreel** *(Page 31, Ex. B.2)*
2. First revelation received by Prophet (ﷺ): **Surat al-Alaq** *(Page 31, Ex. B.1)*
3. Total number of surahs in the Qur'an: **114** *(Page 31, Ex. B.3)*
4. Language of the Holy Qur'an: **Arabic** *(Page 31, Ex. B.4)*
5. Time taken for the Qur'an to be revealed: **23 years** *(Page 31, Ex. B.5)*
6. Mountain where the Cave of Hira is located: **Mount An-Noor** *(Page 29)*
7. Holy Book given to Prophet Musa (عليه السلام): **Tawrah** *(Page 27)*
8. Arabic word for 'guidance' mentioned in the chapter: **Hidaayah** *(Page 29)*
- *Features:* Lightbulb hint buttons (`💡 Need a hint?`) and instant validation.

### 🎯 Section C: Drag-and-Drop & Tap Matching Challenge (12 Marks)
- **Activity 1 (4 Marks):** Match the 4 Prophets with their revealed Holy Books (Musa ➔ Tawrah, Daawood ➔ Zaboor, Isa ➔ Injeel, Muhammad ﷺ ➔ Qur'an).
- **Activity 2 (4 Marks):** Match Chapter Milestones & Facts (First Revelation, Second Revelation, Prophet's Age, Purity of Text).
- **Activity 3 (4 Marks):** Fill-in-the-Blanks Sentence Builder (aayaat, purified, learn, Ramadan).

## 🚀 How to Run

### Option 1: Open Directly in Any Browser
Simply double-click `index.html` to open it in Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox.

### Option 2: Run via Local HTTP Server
```bash
python -m http.server 8080
```
Then visit `http://localhost:8080` in your web browser.

### Option 3: Deploy to GitHub Pages
1. Go to your GitHub repository **Settings**.
2. Click on **Pages** in the left sidebar.
3. Under **Branch**, select `main` and `/ (root)`, then click **Save**.
4. Your interactive quiz will be live worldwide!
